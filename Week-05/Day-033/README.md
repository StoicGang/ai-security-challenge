### How I Approached It
<!-- Numbered steps, briefly. This is the only "steps" section — everything else below should be about the mechanism, not the process. -->
1. Reviewed the Day 32 checklist and identified areas that were too specific to my project.
2. Reorganized the checklist into reusable categories based on different trust relationships, such as AI Models, Datasets, Software  Dependencies, External AI Services, Infrastructure & Deployment, and Governance.
3. Added objective verification evidence and standardized audit statuses to make the checklist practical for evaluating any AI project.

### Key Learning
<!-- The one thing you understand now that you didn't before today. -->
A secure AI supply chain is not limited to AI models. Every external dependency, including datasets, libraries, APIs, containers, and deployment infrastructure, becomes part of the project's trust boundary

### What I'd Do Differently
<!-- If you redid today, what would you change? -->
If I were repeating today's challenge, I would combine the concepts learned from the TryHackMe AI Supply Chain Security room, especially SBOMs, dependency hardening, and version pinning, with the checklist from the beginning.
